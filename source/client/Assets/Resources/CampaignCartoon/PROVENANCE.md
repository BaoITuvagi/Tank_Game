# Campaign cartoon artwork

These transparent PNG atlases were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-11-05 for this project. They are new campaign assets; original `CampaignArt` files and third-party licenses are retained unchanged. Shared `ArenaArt` textures and `TankPalette` remain covered by the existing Kenney license.

- `scenery.png`: 1254×1254, 4×4 fixed cells, 16 scenery sprites.
- `actors.png`: 1774×887, 4×2 fixed square cells, three enemy roles and four bosses; cell 3 is intentionally empty.
- `catalog.json`: original pixel dimensions and padded crop bounds measured inside those cells. Crops alter texture UVs, never tile coordinates, collision, map bytes or action timing. PNGs were copied without raster edits and retain generated alpha. Bilinear sampling uses a half-texel inset.
- Pickup symbols reuse `CartoonUi` icons. Roads and bridges preserve connectivity masks and use code-native geometry; water shares the multiplayer palette and ripple style with cardinal and diagonal shores.

## Final prompt set

### Scenery generation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D overhead cartoon tank-game sprite atlas. Create exactly one transparent PNG sheet, square 1024×1024, strict 4 columns × 4 rows of equal 256×256 invisible cells. Each sprite is centered at the exact center of its cell, contained inside 80% cell bounds with transparent gutters; nothing crosses cell boundaries. No cell borders, no labels, no grid lines. Visual style: clean Kenney-like 2D cartoon military assets, very simple flat shaded color regions, thick charcoal outline, muted olive greens, warm sand concrete, desaturated red roofs, small shadow down-right. True 90-degree overhead orthographic viewpoint; show roofs, no front façades, no isometric perspective. Legible when scaled to 40px. Sixteen separate objects in this exact row-major order: row1: leafy round tree canopy; cluster of three small leafy tree canopies; cluster of angular grey boulders; small house viewed directly above with terracotta gabled roof. row2: large olive military hangar roof; compact industrial factory roof with two small vents; square guard tower roof with battlement rim; small rubble pile of broken grey blocks. row3: parked military cargo truck pointing exactly north/up; olive supply crates cluster; ruined house roof and broken walls; ruined industrial factory roof and blocks. row4: ruined square tower; small planted cream pennant/flag with pole viewed above readable silhouette; yellow-black warning road marking symbol centered on transparent ground; abandoned olive tank pointing north/up. All sprites compact and precisely centered. Background fully transparent. Do not put ground tiles beneath objects. No text, logos, watermarks, decorative framing, gradients, visual noise, pixel art, human characters.

### Enemy and boss generation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite atlas for an overhead 2D cartoon tank combat game. Generate one transparent landscape image with exact 2:1 aspect ratio, strict 4 columns by 2 rows equal invisible square cells. Each sprite centered in its cell with a generous transparent margin, entirely within the center 78% of its cell. Exactly eight sprites. No labels, borders, grid lines or surrounding scenery. All vehicles point exactly straight up/north. True orthographic overhead 90 degree camera; roofs and top armor visible, no front facades, no isometric angle. Style: match simple Kenney 2D top-down tank game: thick charcoal outlines, flat shaded solid-color armor panels, bold simple silhouette, compact tracks, muted grey/olive/red with small warm orange accents, subtle shadow down-right, readable at 40px. Do not render realistic machinery or fine detail. Exact row-major order: row1 cell1 compact red close-combat enemy tank with a short wide cannon and front bumper; cell2 slim charcoal artillery tank with one visibly long red cannon; cell3 olive support vehicle with two short side antennae and a cream wrench emblem on roof, no letters; cell4 empty unused reserve cell, fully transparent. row2: cell1 large burgundy heavy boss tank with two heavy forward cannons; cell2 large dark grey rocket boss carrier with four orange rocket tubes pointing north; cell3 large olive armored boss tank with broad wedge shield at front and one cannon; cell4 large purple command boss tank with circular turret and gold antenna/sensor. Four boss silhouettes must differ strongly. All eight positions obey the uniform cell centers and scale. Background genuinely transparent, no text, logos or watermark, no pixel art, no gradients or highlights outside silhouette.
